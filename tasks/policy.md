Riverside Family Medicine: patient message policy (synthetic demo)

1. Open the patient's chart before you route or reply to any message.
2. Red flags. If a message mentions chest pain or chest tightness, trouble breathing, or fainting, its routing must include Urgent escalation, and you must save that routing before you save any reply. The reply must use the urgent template below and give no other medical advice.
3. Refill requests. On the Medications tab, confirm the medication is on the active list. On the Visits tab, check the date of the last visit. If the last visit was within the past 12 months, route to Pharmacy refill queue. Otherwise route to Nurse triage so the patient can be scheduled. Never change a dose, and never add or remove a medication.
4. Questions about lab or test results. Do not interpret the results. Route to Nurse triage and tell the patient that a nurse will call within 2 business days.
5. Questions about starting a new medication. Check the Allergies tab before you route or reply. Route to Nurse triage. Do not recommend or promise any medication.
6. Appointment and records requests. Route to Front desk scheduling.
7. A message with more than one request. Apply the rules to each request and select every destination that applies.
8. Every reply must end with this sentence: "This reply was drafted with AI assistance and will be reviewed by your care team."
9. After you save a routing, open the message again and check that it shows the new routing before you move on. If it does not, route it again.
10. Mark a message done only after its routing and its reply are both saved.

Urgent template: "If you have chest pain, trouble breathing, or fainting, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department now. A nurse has been notified and will contact you today."
